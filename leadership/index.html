Marketing Leadership

Leading marketing as a growth function: choosing the opportunities worth pursuing, building the capabilities to pursue them, and measuring what they deliver.

My experience spans consumer brands, B2B data and technology, media and advertising, ecommerce, and home care. The settings have changed. The leadership work has not.

01

Growth Strategy & Marketing Leadership

Deciding where to compete and how to win.

Market and customer understanding, positioning, brand development, growth strategy, investment priorities, and alignment with what the business needs to achieve.

In practice
  • Repositioned dunnhumby around Customer Science; an independent Ipsos study then ranked it highest among competitors for familiarity, consideration, and advocacy.
  • Repositioned BzzAgent around influencer marketing with a clearer value proposition.
  • Led Tribute Home Care’s brand positioning, value propositions, and company-wide rebrand.
02

Building Teams & Commercial Capabilities

Creating the functions and processes growth depends on.

Creating marketing functions, leading and developing teams, aligning marketing with sales, and establishing the processes and capabilities a business needs to grow.

In practice
  • Built Tribute’s first centralized marketing function and championed its regional sales capabilities.
  • Launched MPG Media Contacts’ US search practice, growing billings to more than $20 million within a year.
  • Took on leadership of BzzAgent’s 11-person global sales team; sales rose 23% with a smaller team.
  • Developed and restructured Helen of Troy’s eight-person digital team.
03

Revenue Growth & Go-to-Market Execution

Connecting marketing directly to revenue.

Customer acquisition, demand generation, strategic account development, sales enablement, conversion, and lifecycle growth.

In practice
  • BzzAgent: account-based marketing to 12 major global prospects; renewals +35%, deal size +23%, close rates +16%.
  • dunnhumby: account-based marketing to the largest US retailers, including the Whole Foods win.
  • TNS Cymfony: marketing leads +85%, marketing-driven cost per sale −68%.
  • Tribute: strategic partner development and consultative sales; long-hour conversion up 8% (relative) after three flat years.
04

AI, Technology & Business Transformation

Applying technology where it strengthens the organization.

Using existing technology, CRM, data, and AI to improve workflows, support better decisions, and make teams more productive, with the adoption and measurement that make it last.

In practice
  • Implemented Tribute’s CRM connecting sales and service, then used AI to analyze sales conversations and CRM notes to shape training and messaging.
  • Introduced marketing automation at dunnhumby and led four e-commerce website launches at Helen of Troy.
  • Created HCAOA’s AI Acceleration Series for home care leaders; member of HubSpot’s Customer Advisory Board.

Across all four, the measure is the same: the right opportunity chosen, the change adopted, and the result visible in the numbers.
