04
Caregiver Recruiting and Engagement

Give caregivers a reason to choose you—and stay engaged through hiring

The problem

Caregivers have many choices, and good candidates drop out between first contact and their first shift.

My approach

Show caregivers why your agency is a good place to work, and stay in touch at every step of hiring.

Example

“Share the Care” tied caregiver referrals to a charitable donation, made for every referral, hired or not.

What I measure: referral volume, hires by source, cost per hire, where candidates drop out

Toolkit+
  • Employment brand, recruiting voice and caregiver personas
  • Social media as a primary hiring channel
  • Culture videos, caregiver stories, interview prep videos and hiring guide
  • Touchpoints that keep candidates close through each step
  • Hiring events, promotions and registrations
