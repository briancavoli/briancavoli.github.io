How I lead

Build the right things. Make them work. Keep getting better.

Three questions shape how I lead: Is this worth building? Will people actually use it? Is it getting better?

  1. Build the right things

    Building what’s next. Recognize which opportunities matter and which capabilities the business actually needs to take advantage of them. Being able to build something isn’t a reason to build it.

  2. Make them work

    Turning strategy into results. Connect marketing, sales, people, process, and technology so new capabilities get adopted and change how the business performs. The work isn’t done at launch. It’s done when performance changes.

  3. Keep getting better

    Building for continuous improvement. Set meaningful measures, learn from actual results, and use data, feedback, and AI to make systems more effective over time.
