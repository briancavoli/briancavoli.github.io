My perspective

Building the Next Generation of Home Care

Stronger businesses.Better jobs.Better care.

Home care is entering a new era of opportunity.

AI and emerging technologies have the potential to transform how agencies operate, understand their businesses, support caregivers, and serve clients.

But the opportunity isn’t the technology itself. It’s how we apply it.

By connecting data, technology, people, and processes, we can build smarter workflows, uncover insights that were previously out of reach, help teams become more effective, and create a continuous cycle of learning and improvement.

The real opportunity is to build organizations that get better at what they do, every day.

That’s where I see tremendous potential for the future of home care—and where I’m especially excited to contribute.
