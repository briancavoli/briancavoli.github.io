05
Client Experience and Reputation

Turn client feedback into better experiences and stronger recommendations

The problem

Concerns surface too late, happy clients rarely say so publicly, and relationships end when care ends.

My approach

Listen early and often, act on what you hear, and invite satisfied families to share their experience.

Example

Surveys at three weeks and at the end of care flagged concerns early; top scorers received a personal request for a Google review.

What I measure: Net Promoter Score, survey response rate, review volume and rating, returning clients and referrals

Toolkit+
  • Client surveys with Net Promoter Score
  • Personal outreach for Google reviews
  • Monthly emails to former clients, tailored to why care ended, from their account contact
  • Path-to-purchase surveys: influences, values, wish list
