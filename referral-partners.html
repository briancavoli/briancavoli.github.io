Referral Partner Development

Build referral relationships that produce repeat business

The problem

Partner meetings happen, but too few turn into steady referrals, and top partners quietly drift away.

My approach

Manage each partner as a relationship that moves through stages, with a clear next step at each one.

Example

Quarterly account plans and a monthly partner dashboard showed which relationships were growing or slipping, so the team knew where to focus.

What I measure: first and repeat referrals, referrers per partner, revenue and length of stay by partner, relationship health over time

Toolkit+
  • Partners grouped by value: core partners, developing partners, targets
  • Quarterly account plans with stakeholder maps
  • CRM linking partner activity to referrals and revenue
  • Postcard series, role-specific one-pagers, co-branded flyers, partner landing pages
  • Quarterly reviews and continuing-education events for partner staff
