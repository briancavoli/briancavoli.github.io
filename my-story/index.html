My Story

A Career of Building What’s Next

From the early internet to AI-enabled growth.

I’ve always been drawn to what’s coming next.

Throughout my career, some of the most exciting opportunities have emerged when technology changed how businesses could connect with customers, understand their markets, and drive growth.

And I’ve been fortunate to help organizations turn those opportunities into new capabilities and measurable results.

  1. 1990sEarly Internet
  2. 2000sPerformance Marketing
  3. 2000sSocial & Customer Intelligence
  4. 2010sCustomer Science
  5. 2010sDigital Transformation
  6. 2020sAI-Enabled Growth
  1. 1990sMonster · Carat

    The early internet: Building a new way to reach customers

    At Monster, I helped support the rapid growth of one of the internet’s pioneering businesses, building demand generation and sales enablement capabilities for its expanding sales organization.

    At Carat, I led interactive media initiatives for major brands including RadioShack and Pfizer. As new opportunities emerged in streaming media, mobile, and interactive television, I developed the Innovation Engine to help clients explore and capitalize on them. That work led to my promotion to VP of Innovations.

  2. 2000sMPG Media Contacts

    Performance marketing: Turning digital into measurable growth

    As search advertising emerged, I joined MPG Media Contacts (now part of Havas) to launch its US search marketing practice.

    Within a year, we built a team and grew billings to more than $20 million, supported by substantial improvements in client performance.

    The opportunity wasn’t simply another advertising channel. It was a new way to connect marketing investments directly to business outcomes.

  3. 2000sTNS Cymfony

    Social media: Understanding what customers were saying

    As social media transformed how people shared experiences and influenced brands, I joined TNS Cymfony, an early leader in social media intelligence.

    We repositioned the business, rebuilt its marketing capabilities, and used content and customer insights to demonstrate the value of this emerging discipline.

    The results included an 85% increase in marketing leads and a 68% reduction in marketing-driven cost per sale.

    Our innovative work measuring social conversations also received the AMEC Grand Prix award for measurement innovation.

  4. 2010sdunnhumby · BzzAgent · Helen of Troy

    Customer intelligence and digital transformation

    At dunnhumby and BzzAgent, I helped organizations turn customer science, content marketing, influencer marketing, and marketing automation into stronger commercial capabilities. Read the full story.

    Later, at Helen of Troy, I helped modernize digital marketing across PUR, Honeywell, Braun, and Vicks, introducing new practices, developing the team, and leading the launch of four e-commerce-ready websites.

  5. 2020sTribute Home Care

    AI: Building the next generation of growth

    Most recently, at Tribute Home Care, I built a centralized marketing function and progressively expanded its contribution to sales effectiveness, strategic partner development, business intelligence, and AI-enabled growth systems. Read the full story.

    AI opened new opportunities to understand customer behavior, analyze business performance, improve workflows, and develop more effective teams.

    But the underlying objective remained the same.

The Common Thread

Each wave of innovation brought new tools, new possibilities, and new challenges.

What has interested me most isn’t the technology itself. It’s understanding what becomes possible because of it—and then bringing together the strategy, people, processes, and capabilities to make it work.

Sometimes that means building something from scratch. Sometimes it means reinventing an established approach. And sometimes it means helping an organization recognize an opportunity it hadn’t fully appreciated.

The constant has been a commitment to turning what’s next into what’s better.

That’s the thinking I bring to the next chapter of my career.
