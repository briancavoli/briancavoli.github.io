03
Pricing and Revenue Performance

Make pricing decisions with better evidence

The problem

Rates get set by instinct, and discounts given to win a client stay in place long after they should end.

My approach

Look at pricing three ways: where you sit in the market, how price affects conversion, and what families and partners say about cost.

Example

A monthly review put every below-rate client on a timed path back to standard rates, region by region.

What I measure: average billing rate vs. budget, clients below standard rates, discount depth and duration

Toolkit+
  • Competitive research: direct calls plus a managed research firm
  • Pricing recommendations by region
  • Discount policy: when, how deep, how long
  • Billing rate per hour managed to goal and budget
