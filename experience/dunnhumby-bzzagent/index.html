Experience & Impact / dunnhumby & BzzAgent

Reinventing Marketing to Accelerate Growth

dunnhumby & BzzAgent | Global Marketing Leadership

Transforming brand positioning, building modern marketing capabilities, and connecting marketing more directly to business performance.

My experience at dunnhumby and BzzAgent included several opportunities to build and reinvent marketing functions as the businesses evolved.

Although the challenges differed, the common objective was consistent: create more sophisticated marketing capabilities that strengthened differentiation, supported sales, and improved commercial performance.

Building Marketing at dunnhumby

Following dunnhumby’s acquisition of BzzAgent, I was asked to help establish a more sophisticated marketing function for the global data science company, initially leading marketing in the United States.

Despite its scale and relationships with leading retailers and consumer brands, dunnhumby had relatively limited market visibility.

We repositioned the business around Customer Science, developing a stronger brand narrative supported by content marketing, thought leadership, public relations, and an expanded digital presence.

I created a content strategy that generated more than 530 assets across multiple formats over four years, introduced marketing automation, and helped establish practices adopted by teams internationally.

An independent Ipsos brand study subsequently ranked dunnhumby highest among its competitors for familiarity, consideration, and advocacy.

My responsibilities expanded into strategic account development, including account-based marketing targeting the largest US retailers. I also led the coordination of major sales proposals, including the successful pursuit of Whole Foods, the company’s largest new account win in more than five years.

Rebuilding BzzAgent for Growth

I later returned to BzzAgent as Head of Global Marketing, joining a leadership team tasked with revitalizing the business across North America, the UK, and Brazil.

The brand needed renewed differentiation, a clearer value proposition, and a more effective approach to generating demand.

I developed an integrated marketing strategy, rebuilt the website and sales tools, repositioned the offering around influencer marketing, and introduced personalized account-based marketing programs targeting 12 major global prospects.

We also strengthened our evidence of effectiveness through client case studies, independent measurement, and prominent industry media coverage.

Following the marketing transformation

Business Impact

  • +35%

    Improvement in client renewal rates.

  • +23%

    Increase in average media product deal size.

  • +16%

    Increase in sales close rates versus the previous year.

I also transformed the social media strategy for a community of 1.5 million members, increasing engagement 76% and impressions 106% in the first year.

During the company’s subsequent sale process, I assumed additional leadership of its 11-person global sales team. Sales increased 23% compared with the previous period, despite a smaller team.

What This Experience Demonstrates

These experiences reinforced the importance of building marketing around a clear understanding of customers, compelling differentiation, and measurable business performance.

They also demonstrated the value of creating systems that scale: integrated content strategies, marketing automation, personalized account development, coordinated sales enablement, and stronger connections between marketing and revenue.

The lesson I’ve carried forward is simple:

Marketing creates the greatest value when it combines creative differentiation with the capabilities to consistently turn that differentiation into business results.
