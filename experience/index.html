Experience & Results

I’ve repeatedly recognized what was coming next in marketing and technology, built the capabilities to capitalize on it, and helped organizations turn innovation into business performance.

The pattern

Three themes across my career

Consumer brands, B2B data and technology, media, ecommerce, and home care. The industries changed; the leadership work stayed consistent.

01

Building organizations and capabilities

Creating the teams, functions, processes, and systems a business needs for its next stage of growth.

  • Tribute Home Care. Built the company’s first centralized marketing function across multiple locations, implemented its CRM, and served on the Executive Committee.
  • MPG Media Contacts. Launched the US search marketing practice, building the team and growing billings to more than $20 million within a year.
  • dunnhumby. Established a more sophisticated US marketing function for the global data science company, with practices later adopted by teams internationally.
  • Helen of Troy. Developed an eight-person digital team and modernized digital marketing across PUR, Honeywell, Braun, and Vicks.
02

Driving growth and performance

Measurable improvements in customer acquisition, revenue, efficiency, and commercial results.

  • Tribute Home Care. Company revenue more than doubled organically during my tenure, from $17 million in 2021 to a projected $35 million in 2026, with strong 2026 gains in partner revenue and long-hour conversion.
  • BzzAgent. Following the marketing transformation, client renewals rose 35%, average deal size 23%, and close rates 16%. After I took on leadership of the 11-person global sales team, sales rose 23% with a smaller team.
  • TNS Cymfony. Marketing leads grew 85% while marketing-driven cost per sale fell 68%.
  • dunnhumby. Led coordination of the Whole Foods pursuit, the company’s largest new account win in more than five years.
  • Monster. Built demand generation and sales enablement capabilities for a rapidly expanding sales organization.
03

Leading innovation and transformation

Recognizing change early, adopting new approaches, and helping organizations move forward.

  • Carat. Led interactive media for brands including RadioShack and Pfizer and created the Innovation Engine for streaming, mobile, and interactive TV, leading to my promotion to VP of Innovations.
  • TNS Cymfony. Helped establish social media intelligence as a discipline; our measurement work received the AMEC Grand Prix for measurement innovation.
  • dunnhumby & BzzAgent. Repositioned brands around Customer Science and influencer marketing, and introduced marketing automation and account-based marketing.
  • Helen of Troy. Led the launch of four e-commerce-ready brand websites.
  • Tribute Home Care. Applied AI to sales conversations and CRM data, and turned the findings into training, messaging, and Growth Systems.

A Career of Building What’s Next

Each wave of innovation brought new tools and new challenges. What has interested me most isn’t the technology itself. It’s understanding what becomes possible because of it, then bringing together the strategy, people, and processes to make it work.

Sometimes that means building something from scratch. Sometimes it means reinventing an established approach. And sometimes it means helping an organization see an opportunity it hadn’t fully appreciated.
