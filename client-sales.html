02
Client Sales Conversion

Help more families move from inquiry to care

The problem

Families call during stressful moments. Without a consistent approach, good inquiries are lost and coaching relies on guesswork.

My approach

Give the team a clear method for guiding families to an informed decision, and use AI to learn from every real conversation.

Example

AI analysis of call transcripts surfaced families’ real concerns; selected call clips became coaching examples and shaped follow-up.

What I measure: conversion by rep and inquiry type, long-hour care wins, follow-up completion, reasons families choose care or don’t

Toolkit+
  • Training: brand value, family personas, qualifying, concerns, next steps
  • CRM inquiry workflow, referral source tracking, alerts
  • Rep dashboards: conversion trends, strengths, development areas
  • Follow-up emails, referral thank-yous, educational pages for families
  • Videos featuring team members and caregivers
